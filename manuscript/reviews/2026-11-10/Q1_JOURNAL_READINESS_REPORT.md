The best submission direction centers the certificate-evidence problem and its falsifiable contracts. Broad GNN superiority, universal attack detection, causal attribution from time alone, and optimal autonomous remediation would overstate the artifact. Current revisions retain the article's styling, title, section structure, figures and layer count while making the scientific claims match the evidence.

The work should continue with installed current-patch validation and matched external provenance tasks. Literal bilinear implementation, stronger whole-family splits, and explicit false-order/correlated-source controls now have executed evidence; their remaining scientific issue is demonstrating benefit under a justified deployment contract. These are research tasks, rather than cosmetic revisions. A submission-ready decision should be revisited after their results and the final compiled artifact are synchronized.
