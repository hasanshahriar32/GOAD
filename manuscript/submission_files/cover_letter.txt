Subject: CertGraph: Cryptographic Invariant Provenance and Multiplicative Bilinear Gating for Active Directory Certificate Services Escalation Interdiction

Dear Editor,

We propose CertGraph as a study of certificate-specific evidence assurance in Active Directory Certificate Services. The framework combines static graph analysis, an issuer-scoped provenance reference, and budgeted interdiction. Its central empirical question is when incomplete or conflicting evidence supports a possible or definite issuance-to-use explanation.

The artifact includes a frozen synthetic confirmation and component studies, four final twelve-trial Windows matrices, and executed Certipy/Sigma configuration-risk comparisons. The matrices verify 42 successful exact-certificate authentications and six certificate-bound KDC rejections, including nine administrative uses. Windows 2019 build 17763.9376 request-form controls distinguish UPN-only rejection from three SID-URL administrative uses after template restoration, when configuration scans cease flagging ESC1. Matched requests are identical within each block and form; the old/new workflows differ, so these results do not isolate a patch-only causal effect. Missing trial CA exports are transparently supplemented by a later recorded read-only snapshot.

Additional matched model and exhaustive small-graph audits identify neural horizon errors, augmentation confounding, and heuristic optimality gaps. A separate literal two-layer bilinear implementation has 10,800 independently reproduced held-out predictions on family-disjoint inputs, without a classification advantage over matched additive attention. Equal-task projection measurements retain every queried pair and include projection cost. The manuscript does not claim universal GNN superiority, an optimal Stackelberg solver, or enterprise deployment effectiveness from laboratory evidence.

The final cover letter must be reviewed by all authors after journal selection and after the experiments, submission declarations, and public artifact release are finalized. Originality, exclusive submission, author approval, and conflicts must be confirmed factually before submitting this draft. No submission is made by this file.
