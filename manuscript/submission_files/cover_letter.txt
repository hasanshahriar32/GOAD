Subject: CertGraph: Cryptographic Invariant Provenance and Multiplicative Bilinear Gating for Active Directory Certificate Services Escalation Interdiction

Dear Editor,

We propose CertGraph as a study of certificate-specific evidence assurance in Active Directory Certificate Services. The framework combines static graph analysis, an issuer-scoped provenance reference, and budgeted interdiction. Its central empirical question is when incomplete or conflicting evidence supports a possible or definite issuance-to-use explanation.

The artifact includes a frozen synthetic confirmation and component studies, two paired twelve-trial Windows matrices with exact-certificate authentication outcomes, and executed Certipy/Sigma configuration-risk comparisons. Matched signed requests distinguish the tested configuration intervention from baseline and rollback-before-enrollment conditions. The measured Windows 2019 build is explicitly reported; its base results are not described as current-patch validation.

Additional matched model and exhaustive small-graph audits identify neural horizon errors, augmentation confounding, and heuristic optimality gaps. The manuscript reports those findings and distinguishes its general bilinear formulation from the evaluated feature-channel implementation. It does not claim universal GNN superiority, an optimal Stackelberg solver, or enterprise deployment effectiveness from laboratory evidence.

The final cover letter must be reviewed by all authors after journal selection and after the experiments, submission declarations, and public artifact release are finalized. Originality, exclusive submission, author approval, and conflicts must be confirmed factually before submitting this draft. No submission is made by this file.
