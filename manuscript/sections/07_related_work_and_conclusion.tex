\section{Related Work, Discussion, and Conclusion}
\label{sec:related_conclusion}

\subsection{Related Work}

\paragraph{Active Directory and PKI Security:}
BloodHound~\cite{robbins2017bloodhound} models identity relationships for privilege-path investigation. SpecterOps~\cite{schroeder2021certified} systematized ADCS misconfigurations; Certipy~\cite{lyak2021certipy} supports certificate-service assessment. A restored configuration snapshot alone cannot reveal every intervening issuance. However, practical CA/KDC correlation is established: In.security describes SAN-abuse investigation in KQL~\cite{insecurity2023adcs}, and Splunk publishes an ESC1 authentication analytic~\cite{splunk2026esc1}. Recent vendor investigations also document certificate-backed intrusion paths and multi-event investigation practices~\cite{microsoft2026adcsintrusions,unit422026adcs}. These are operational reports, rather than controlled comparisons with this implementation. CIPG therefore does not claim novelty for joining issuance and authentication events. Its research question concerns certificate-specific identity consistency, possible versus definite answers, and revision under incomplete or conflicting evidence.

\paragraph{Graph Neural Networks in Cyber Defense:}
GNNs have been applied to vulnerability discovery, binary analysis, and intrusion detection~\cite{kipf2017semi,velickovic2018graph,guo2022practical,king2023euler,goel2024optimizing}. In access-control multigraphs, aggregation can lose intrinsic features under the assumptions of Theorem~\ref{thm:theorem1}. Learned gates and residual paths do not establish discrete authorization reachability. Our matched audit separates augmentation from the named implementation contrast and finds a false-positive/false-negative trade-off rather than general gate superiority. Its feature-channel implementation also differs from the general bilinear formulation. Exact symbolic analysis remains a separate component, and checking only neural positives would miss the observed out-of-horizon positives.

\paragraph{Provenance Graphs and Causality Tracking:}
System provenance techniques such as CamFlow~\cite{pasquier2017practical}, Unicorn~\cite{han2020unicorn}, and NodeMerge~\cite{hassan2020tactical} analyze host activities. HADES~\cite{liu2026hades} explicitly extends investigation to whole-network AD provenance, while CPD~\cite{liu2026cpd} addresses persistence. We do not infer that these systems are incapable of detecting a certificate-related attack from their different emphasis; a controlled comparison would require executing them with appropriate telemetry. Possible/certain answers for partially ordered data~\cite{amarilli2017order}, temporal constraint networks~\cite{dechter1991temporal}, and entity resolution under inconsistent evidence~\cite{fagin2023entity} also predate this work. The recent time-model SoK~\cite{heinzl2026timestamps} is a preprint that directly motivates making clock, delivery, and evidence assumptions explicit. Our proposed contribution is their certificate-specific integration and evaluated evidence contract, with no priority claim for the constituent reasoning techniques.

\paragraph{Game-Theoretic Interdiction and Access Control Defense:}
Network interdiction models have been extensively studied in game theory and physical infrastructure defense~\cite{tambe2011security,dahlhaus1994multiterminal,garg1994multiway}. In identity security, Guo et al.~\cite{guo2022practical,guo2023scalable} analyzed shortest-path interdiction on BloodHound graphs, which was subsequently generalized to limited-query edge blocking and choke-point analysis~\cite{guo2024limited,zhang2025rethinking} and optimal decoy placement via Stackelberg games~\cite{ngo2024catch,ngo2024decoy}. Theorem~\ref{thm:nphardness} specializes known directed multiway-cut hardness to the stated general pair-interdiction formulation; it is not a new graph-complexity result. Non-blockable edges and assigned disruption weights follow established interdiction concerns. The evaluated adaptive method is a sequential heuristic. A new exhaustive small-instance audit identifies suboptimal cases and separates a metadata-preserving correction from historical simulation results. Neither the formulation nor its label supplies an equilibrium or optimal-remediation certificate.

\subsection{Discussion and Operational Considerations}
\paragraph{Deployment in 24/7 Enterprise SOC Environments:}
The legacy synthetic replay reports 13,192.4 events/sec and 6.91 MB on a fixed stream. Those measurements do not establish zero interference with domain authentication, bounded history storage, or end-to-end CA polling cost. The new lineage reference recomputes retained evidence, and the fault-budget reference enumerates deletion hypotheses. The new live command envelopes and offline inference timings quantify two parts of this cost: median recorded trial envelopes are 50.91~s and 32.14~s in the two environments, while median offline identity reasoning is 10.99~ms and 9.18~ms. They do not measure continuous polling or authentication interference.

\paragraph{Cloud-Hybrid Identity Extensions:}
Modern enterprises increasingly synchronize Active Directory with cloud-based Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure AD) via Entra Connect. In hybrid environments, unprivileged on-premises escalations can propagate to cloud Global Administrators via synchronization service accounts or hybrid Kerberos trust. Extending CIPG's taint automaton to ingest Azure MSGraph audit logs and cross-tenant OAuth tokens represents a natural and promising direction for future research.

\subsection{Threats to Validity and Limitations}
We distinguish the following threats to validity and experimental limitations:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Evidence Scale and Label Validity:} The archive's scenario names do not establish seven successful attacks. Four final fixed-request matrices supply 42 successful exact-certificate authentications and six certificate-bound rejections, with nine privileged uses and matched self-use controls. They establish controlled outcomes, not enterprise prevalence. Independent Windows 2019 builds 17763.4974 and 17763.9376 are measured. Current-patch controls reveal request-form differences, but the additional post-rollback workflow prevents attributing every old/new difference solely to the update. Eighteen missing contemporaneous CA exports are explicitly supplemented by a later read-only snapshot; original captures remain intact. Original source images remain read-only.
    \item \textbf{Static Model and Generator Validity:} The matched audit corrects reachable-path labels and checks 6,000 checkpoint predictions. Repeated training/validation archetypes, fixed first-step transforms, hand-initialized projections, and the distinction between feature-channel and bilinear gating restrict what its numbers establish. A separate literal bilinear study removes those implementation and repeated-whole-input problems with trainable encoders and typed family-disjoint splits. Both literal bilinear and additive attention still miss all 450 long-path positives per arm, without a classification advantage for gating. A verifier restricted to neural positives cannot repair these misses, and symbolic label agreement is not independent Windows ground truth.
    \item \textbf{Parametric Disruption Costs vs. Interactive Administrator Feedback:} In our Stackelberg remediation game, operational disruption costs $c(e)$ are assigned parametrically according to relation type ($c(\text{MemberOf}) \in [2.8, 3.8], c(\text{GenericAll}) \in [2.0, 3.0], c(\text{Enroll}) \in [1.0, 1.4]$) and operational edge immutability constraints ($E_b \subsetneq E$). In production enterprise deployments, operational criticality is context-sensitive and evolving. The exact small-instance audit gives concrete optimum comparisons rather than a universal heuristic guarantee. Integrating real-time human-in-the-loop preference elicitation~\cite{guo2024limited} represents a fruitful path for future enhancement.
    \item \textbf{Trust, Time, and Necessary Causation:} Candidate retention under a bridge fault budget assumes correct raw issuance/KDC records and captured true issuance. Consistently forged raw histories defeat the methods, while faults exceeding the declared budget invalidate its retention guarantee. A new counterexample shows that self-consistent false order can also produce a wrong definite overlap. A grouped-source reference handles the six-record correlated-export control under an explicit one-source assumption, which differs from a record budget. The operation-order pilot uses the controlled orchestrator as an additional trusted source and does not repair the failed Windows audit-clock model. Temporal overlap alone does not establish necessity. The new matched CSR/key interventions support a conditional contrast under the recorded policy fields and controlled CA refresh, while unobserved concurrent changes and missing restoration audit events remain distinct evidence concerns.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Conclusion}
CertGraph combines static graph analysis, certificate-level provenance, and disruption-budgeted interdiction. Its strongest new evidence is the explicit issuer-scoped lineage contract and the controlled connection between issued public certificates, CA records, and successful KDC identity outcomes. Four final live matrices validate 42 successful uses and six rejections. Current-patch SID-URL certificates support three administrative uses after rollback, when all tested-template Certipy scans cease flagging ESC1. Actual Certipy and Sigma execution separates risky configuration from privileged use and malicious intent. The matched intervention evidence is conditional on recorded inputs; the controlled order channel is distinct from audit-clock calibration.

The synthetic confirmation and component studies quantify candidate retention, certain attribution, optional-source faults, and explicit ordering requirements. The static-model audits include a separate literal bilinear implementation, family-disjoint inputs and 10,800 independently reproduced held-out predictions; they find no classification gain from gating and identify deep-path false negatives. Exhaustive small-graph interdiction checks expose heuristic optimality gaps, while a path-preserving projection provides an equal-task timing comparison. These results preserve the framework's existing components while replacing universal cure, causal, and optimality claims with reproducible evidence and clearly stated contracts. They support further evaluation of certificate-specific uncertainty-aware investigation; general deployment effectiveness is not inferred from laboratory or generator results.
