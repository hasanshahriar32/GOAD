## Completed evidence

Six legacy runs and three paired-task runs, seeds 11/29/47. The result-integrity audit checked 18 prediction sets and 5,400 test rows. Five semantic/integrity unit tests pass. Tables/figures derive from saved JSON, with their source hashes recorded. Original journal source/PDF/DOCX are archived here as `manuscript_before_repair.*`; historical full thesis and presentation remain uncorrected.

Key findings: mixed-training GNN mean macro-F1 99.71%, count RF 99.90%, generator diagnostic 100%. Paired IID GNN accuracy 89.56%, count RF 95%; remote membership all learned methods 50% case accuracy and 0% complete-pair accuracy. GNN+reference averages 407.31 ms per 300-case IID corpus versus 2.89 ms for the reference alone. These support a diagnostic benchmark contribution, not the earlier superiority narrative.

## What would materially strengthen submission

1. Independently label actual Windows/ADCS cases with collected configuration, effective-permission evidence and observed enrollment/authentication outcomes. A live configuration snapshot is useful but is not an exploitability label.
2. Add environment- and time-disjoint evaluation across more than one independently configured environment. Multiple seeds or copies of GOAD are not independent organizations.
3. Compare actual version-pinned Certipy and BloodHound on identical snapshots. Map outputs to an explicitly principal-specific task; document unsupported cases and collection failures.
4. Expand interventions beyond this simple ESC13 abstraction, including alternate paths, raw deny/inheritance semantics, missing data and overlapping weaknesses. Keep families together.
5. Establish a distinctive result: a broadly useful validated benchmark, or a measured advantage under incomplete observations/scale. Merely adding another GNN or increasing synthetic sample count will not establish novelty.

Current recommendation: use the repaired draft for supervisor review and experiment planning; do not submit it as the original claimed production detection/autonomous-defense breakthrough. Check target venue scope, current quartile category/year and AI disclosure requirements before submission. The earlier audit includes verified primary sources and venue caveats.
